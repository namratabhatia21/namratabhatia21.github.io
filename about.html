About

Hi, I'm Namrata.

I build AI systems people can trust. Before that, I spent years helping people make sense of systems they didn't build.

I studied physics at the University of Delhi, then moved into instructional design and technical writing. At NIIT, Accenture and Novartis I turned complex software into training and documentation that people could actually use. The biggest was an SAP S/4HANA and Ariba rollout for more than 3,000 employees, where I led a team of 15 in Sydney.

At Novartis I started measuring what that training did, with Qlik Sense, Power BI and SQL. The data pulled me in. In 2023 I went back to study, and did an MSc in Data Science & AI at emlyon business school and McGill University, finishing with a 4.0 GPA, with client projects at Prosol and Fastned along the way.

Since 2024 I've been at Alstom, building production LLM systems: classification, retrieval and agents that review engineering requirements.

My background is my edge. I know where systems break for the people using them, so I design for that from the start.

How I work

  1. 01

    Simple beats clever

    I test the simple option first and ship it if the evaluation says it's enough. A small BERT model beats an LLM for high-volume classification.

  2. 02

    Observable by default

    Tracing, retries and dashboards ship with version one. If nobody can see what a system is doing, it isn't finished.

  3. 03

    People make the final call

    AI proposes and people decide. Their decisions become the next evaluation set.
